# Contoso Contact Center Flash Promo Design

## Style Prompt
High-energy Contoso Bank command-center promo: midnight navy canvas, neon cyan and emerald data light, amber human-risk moments, kinetic type, glass telemetry cards, scanlines, wipes, and clean cinematic audio.

## Colors
- Midnight: `#050914`
- Command blue: `#08152f`
- Cyan: `#38bdf8`
- Sky: `#7dd3fc`
- Emerald: `#34d399`
- Amber: `#fbbf24`
- Rose: `#fb7185`
- Violet: `#a78bfa`

## Typography
Inter/system sans-serif with heavy tabular numerals for telemetry.

## Motion Rules
Flashy but controlled: neon cover wipes, fast metric punches, scanlines, data beams, breathing pauses, and gentle cinematic audio effects. No harsh screeching or overprocessed echo.
